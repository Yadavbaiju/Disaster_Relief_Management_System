# Disaster Relief Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Disaster Relief Management System** is a software application designed to help organize and manage disaster relief operations efficiently. It aims to simplify the coordination of relief resources, track essential supplies, and support the distribution of assistance to people affected by natural or man-made disasters.

The system provides a centralized approach to managing disaster-related information, helping improve resource utilization, coordination, and response efficiency.

## 🎯 Objectives

- To improve the management and coordination of disaster relief activities.
- To maintain organized records of disaster-related information and relief resources.
- To help manage the availability and distribution of essential supplies.
- To reduce manual work and improve the efficiency of relief operations.
- To support faster and more organized disaster response.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Disaster Information Management:** Organize information related to disaster events.
- **Resource Management:** Maintain records of essential relief materials and their availability.
- **Relief Distribution:** Support the allocation and distribution of relief supplies.
- **Data Management:** Store and manage relevant records systematically.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Simplify relief management through a structured software solution.
